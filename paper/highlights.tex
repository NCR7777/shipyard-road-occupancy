\begin{highlights}
\item Roads are modelled as resources held for blocking times with load-dependent widths
\item A certified bound holds for every rule that respects widths and timers, on any route
\item Yard capacity runs from a provably deadlock-free reservation rule to the system bound
\item The ratio of demand to the system bound shows when road occupancy changes the fleet
\item A vehicle waiting for a crane on a single-lane dock road cuts off the stops beyond it
\end{highlights}
